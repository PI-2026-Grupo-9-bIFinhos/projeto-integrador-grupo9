About Us:

Pedro:

Birth:

My life’s journey began sixteen years ago, when my mother discovered she was pregnant. The news brought a sense of great anticipation to the entire family, who eagerly awaited my arrival. On July 23, 2010, I was born three weeks premature. As the firstborn, my arrival was an unforgettable milestone for everyone in the household.

What happened on the day I was born:

Venezuelan President Hugo Chávez announced the severing of diplomatic relations with Colombia, after the Colombian government accused Venezuela of harboring FARC guerrillas within its territory.

Educational Journey:

My educational path began in 2013, when I enrolled at the CMEI (Early Childhood Education Center) in Vila Nova. In 2014, I started attending the Osvaldo Cruz Municipal School. A major change occurred in 2018, when I enrolled at Colégio La Salle Toledo, where I studied until the 9th grade, finishing in 2024. In 2025, I attended the João Arnaldo Ritt Civic-Military School, where I completed the first year of high school.

During 2026, I am repeating the first year at IFPR, where I am enrolled in a technical program for Internet Informatics, integrated with my high school education.

Current situation

Studying at IFPR, I have access to high-quality education; I am very happy at the institute, and I have already been able to determine which field I want to pursue.

Future prospects:

My plan is to complete high school at IFPR, and I intend to pursue a degree in Bioprocess Engineering.


Where We Live:

Municipality:

I reside in the city of Toledo, Paraná. Before the city was founded, the area served as a trail for drovers and *yerba mate* harvesters. One of their campsites and resting spots was known as "Pouso Toledo." Toledo hosts the famous *Festa Nacional do Porco no Rolete* (National Whole Roasted Pig Festival)—a gastronomic event with over 45 years of tradition and international recognition that draws thousands of visitors to the city. Toledo is a benchmark for infrastructure, featuring an extensive network of paved rural roads, and its agricultural GDP places it at the top of national municipal development rankings.

Neighborhood:

I live in Vila Nova, a village with approximately 2,200 inhabitants.

Of these 2,200 inhabitants, approximately 1,000 live in the rural area—though I do not. One of the village’s landmarks is the Catholic church, which is located across from the town square.
